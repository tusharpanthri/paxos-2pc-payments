Here's how it works click to collapse
  1. You get your own cluster Connecting spins up 3 shards × 3 replicas of a bank ledger just for you. A majority is 2 of 3. Disconnect and it's gone.
  2. Start with the tour Type demo or hit run tour. It types each command for you and explains what to watch for. Ctrl-C stops it.
  3. Writes are Paxos rounds put tushar 100 — the shard leader proposes, a majority accepts, then it commits. Cyan lines are Paxos.
  4. Cross-shard = two-phase commit transfer tushar varun 25 — each shard votes by reaching quorum; unanimous means commit. Magenta lines are 2PC.
  5. Break it kill s2n0 takes a replica down; partition s2n0 | s2n1 s2n2 splits the network. Watch a new leader get elected (yellow).
  6. Fix it and inspect revive s2n0, heal, then status or datastore to confirm every replica agrees. help lists everything.
